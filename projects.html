Chabad of Kiryat Gat

Complete prayer hall furnishing — Aron Kodesh, Bimah, Amud Hazan, benches and shtenders. Walnut finish with gold ornamental highlights. Named dedication plaques for major donors.

Traditional Chassidic interior — monumental Aron Kodesh with carved ornament, raised Bimah platform, full bench rows and matching shtenders in dark walnut.

Premium bespoke collection for a private London synagogue — complete furniture set in light oak with hand-finished details and engraved donor dedication plaques throughout.

Contemporary Aron Kodesh and Bimah in natural ash with clean architectural lines. Designed to integrate with the modern synagogue interior while maintaining halachic proportions.
