Complete Synagogue
Furniture Package

Synagogues often buy furniture in parts — the Aron Kodesh is beautiful, the cabinets are different, the benches are a third style. KI-PRO solves this: one complete set, one style, one wood, one finish. A space that feels intentional, dignified and permanent.

When furniture is designed as one complete system,
the entire space feels more dignified, organized and permanent.

Every piece shares the same wood species, the same finish tone, the same design language and the same level of craftsmanship. The result is not just furniture — it is a sanctuary that reflects the values of the community.
