The Process

From Concept to Sanctuary

Every synagogue furniture project follows a structured process designed to eliminate surprises and deliver exactly what was promised.

01
Design & Documentation

We begin with your floor plan and vision. Our designers produce technical shop drawings, material specifications and 3D concept visuals — the full documentation package your board needs to approve the project with confidence.

02
Material Selection & Samples

We provide physical finish samples before production begins — so you can see and feel the actual wood and stain combination in your synagogue's light. No surprises on delivery day.

03
Handcrafted Production

Our craftsmen cut, shape, join and carve every piece by hand in our workshop. Structural joints are reinforced for public use. Ornamental elements are hand-carved or precision-routed — depending on the design language chosen.

04
Finishing & Quality Control

Each piece is sanded, stained and finished with commercial-grade protective coats engineered for decades of use. Before packaging, every item is inspected against the shop drawings and photographed for your records.

05
Protected International Shipping

Synagogue furniture is fragile in transit. We use custom-built crating, foam padding and rigid corner protection — not generic cardboard. Every shipment is tracked and insured to your destination.

06
Installation & Handover

We coordinate installation through our team or trusted local partners in Israel, UK and USA. Care instructions, maintenance guides and spare hardware are included with every project.

Our Materials

Wood Species & Finishes

The material is the foundation of every piece. We offer a range of premium solid woods and finishes — each appropriate for synagogue environments that demand durability and dignity.

European Oak

The classic choice for synagogue furniture — dense, stable and accepting of any stain. Excellent for carved details and structural pieces alike.

American Walnut

Rich chocolate-brown grain with natural warmth. Preferred for prominent pieces like Aron Kodesh and Bimah where deep, luxurious tone is essential.

Beech

Fine, consistent grain ideal for seating and benches. Takes stain evenly and resists the wear of daily public use exceptionally well.

Cherry

Warm reddish tone that deepens beautifully with age. Preferred for donor dedication pieces and ceremonial items where aging gracefully matters.

Ash

Light, open grain with a distinctive cathedral pattern. Well-suited for contemporary synagogue interiors and large format pieces like bookcases.

Premium Veneer

For large surface areas where solid wood would be impractical, we use real-wood veneer on premium MDF or plywood substrate — never on cheap particleboard.

Our Standards

Built for Public Use —
Not Just Display

Synagogue furniture must endure decades of daily use — hundreds of congregants every week, children, elderly, Torah scrolls. We engineer for this from the very first joint.

  • ✓ Solid wood and premium veneer only — no cheap MDF without veneer or particleboard
  • ✓ Mortise-and-tenon and dowel joinery for all structural connections
  • ✓ Rounded and eased edges on all benches and seating pieces
  • ✓ Commercial lacquer and oil finishes rated for public contact
  • ✓ Hardware — hinges, locks, handles — rated for commercial use cycles
  • ✓ Shop drawings reviewed and approved before production begins
  • ✓ Photo documentation at every production milestone
  • ✓ Custom crating and padding for international freight

Why Ukraine?

Our craftsmen trained in classical furniture production — many with backgrounds in architectural millwork, church woodwork and museum-quality restoration. This tradition translates directly into the level of detail that synagogue furniture demands.

  • Access to premium Central European hardwoods
