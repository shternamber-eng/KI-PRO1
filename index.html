Production Standards

We build furniture not just beautiful on day one, but reliable for prayer and study for many years to come. Every piece is built for public use, not just display.

  • ✓ Solid wood and premium veneer — no cheap substitutes
  • ✓ Reinforced construction engineered for public use
  • ✓ Commercial protective finishes and rounded edges
  • ✓ Shop drawings before production; finish samples before approval
  • ✓ Factory quality control and protected international packaging
  • ✓ Delivery and installation coordination; care instructions provided

Board Approval Support

Board doesn't fear the price. Board fears explaining the price without arguments.

We help synagogue leadership present the project clearly and confidently:

  • Technical specifications and shop drawings
  • Material options and finish samples
  • Visual references and 3D concepts
  • Production timeline and milestones
  • Dedication and donor opportunities
We help you not only produce — but get the project approved by the board with confidence.
International Service

Delivery & Installation Support

Shtern Consulting produces custom synagogue furniture in Ukraine and provides worldwide delivery. For international projects, we coordinate installation through our team or trusted local installation partners, depending on the project location, building requirements and client needs.

Installation support is available in the USA, UK, Israel and Europe through trusted local partners — so you always have a clear point of contact on your project.

Buyers fear not just quality — but who will assemble the furniture, how it will arrive, and what happens if there are questions on site. We provide clear answers to all of this.
